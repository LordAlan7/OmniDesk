## **Design introduction** 

### **Project Name** 

OmniDesk : Centralized IT Service and Knowledge Management Platform 

### **Problem being addressed** 

The organization currently lacks a centralized system for managing technical issues reported by users and stores. Problems are primarily communicated through Outlook email and WhatsApp, making it difficult to consistently register, prioritize, assign, monitor, and document issues throughout their resolution process. 

The current process can result in unclear responsibilities, duplicated efforts, delayed attention to high-priority issues, and limited visibility into the status and history of reported problems. With approximately 15 issues reported per week, the absence of a structured system limits the ability of the IT team to efficiently manage and follow up on these requests. 

### **Solution being designed** 

The proposed solution is a centralized IT Service and Knowledge Management Platform that will provide a structured environment for managing technical issues. 

The platform will allow users to report problems and provide the relevant information needed for their resolution. IT personnel will be able to visualize, prioritize, assign, and track issues through a defined workflow. 

The solution will also centralize technical knowledge and incorporate an AI support assistant capable of providing troubleshooting guidance based on authorized organizational information. The AI component is intended to support IT personnel by making relevant knowledge easier to access during issue resolution. 

### **Design objectives** 

The design has the following objectives: 

- Centralize technical issue management in a single platform instead of relying primarily on distributed communication through email and WhatsApp. 

- Provide traceability for reported issues from their registration through their resolution. 

- Support prioritization and assignment of issues according to the established responsibilities of the IT team. 

- Centralize technical knowledge so that relevant information can be accessed during the resolution process. 

- Integrate AI-assisted support using authorized organizational information. 

##### **Software Projects Management** 

- Provide a modular architecture that separates the main system components and facilitates their development, testing, and maintenance. 

- Apply appropriate security and access controls to protect organizational information and restrict access according to user responsibilities. 

### **Scope of the design** 

The software architecture and design will cover the components and interactions required to support the approved requirements of the IT Service and Knowledge Management Platform. 

The design will include the following areas: 

- Authentication, users, and roles, including authentication, role-based access control, and user/technician management, based on FR-001, FR-002, NFR-001, and NFR-002. 

- Ticket management, including ticket creation, classification, priority management, technician assignment, status tracking, investigation, resolution, and closure. The design will also consider attachments, business information, user ticket tracking, and notifications according to their corresponding priorities. 

- Knowledge management, including a centralized repository for technical problems and solutions, technical manuals, documentation, and recurring problems. 

- AI-assisted technical support, including the technical integration required for the AI assistant, human validation of AI-generated information, and transparency regarding AI-generated recommendations. AI-assisted ticket creation and quotation support will only be included if their corresponding Could Have requirements are approved for implementation. 

- Reports and service metrics, including the architectural support required for dashboards and reports concerning tickets, priorities, deadlines, workload, recurring problems, and support activity. 

- Quality attributes, particularly access control, confidentiality, availability, performance, usability, maintainability, traceability, data integrity, and browser compatibility. Scalability will be considered as a Could Have requirement. 

The design will also consider the dependencies identified in the requirements specification. Authentication is a prerequisite for most system modules; ticket management represents the core of the platform; and the knowledge base provides the foundation for the AI assistant and technical documentation. 

##### **Software Projects Management** 

The design will not introduce additional functionality solely because it could be considered useful. Any functionality not represented by an approved requirement must first be justified as a business/user need, technical constraint, or architectural decision and, where applicable, incorporated through the requirements change process.

## **System architecture** 

### **Architectural Style** 

##### **Selected Architecture: Modular Monolith with Layered Architecture** 

The proposed system will use a Modular Monolith architecture, organized internally following a Layered Architecture and exposed through a Client-Server/API-based communication model. 

##### **Software Projects Management** 

This combination is appropriate because the project is being developed by a small team and contains several closely related functionalities, including authentication, ticket management, knowledge management, AI assistance, and reporting. These functionalities need to share data and business rules, but they do not currently justify the operational complexity of independent microservices.

### **Data protection** 

OmniDesk will protect its data through the following measures: 

- **Controlled access to data:** Users will only be able to access information according to their assigned role and permissions. For example, an employee should not have access to administrative information that is only intended for managers. 

- **Backend protection:** The frontend will not directly access the database. All requests for information will go through the backend, where the system can verify the user's authentication and permissions before accessing the data. 

- **Database integrity:** PostgreSQL will use relationships, constraints, and validation rules to prevent inconsistent or invalid information from being stored. For example, a ticket should maintain valid relationships with its user, assigned technician, and status. 

- **Protection of sensitive information:** Information such as employee accounts, tickets, technical documentation, and audit records should only be accessible to authorized users. 

- **Traceability:** Important changes to the system should be recorded. This includes ticket assignments, status changes, relevant activities, and AIgenerated information. This allows the organization to know what changes were made and helps protect the integrity of the information. 

### **Application security** 

Application security refers to the measures used to protect OmniDesk from unauthorized access, malicious inputs, and attacks that could affect the system or its data. 

##### **Software Projects Management** 

The application should implement the following security measures: 

- **Input validation:** The system must verify the information entered by users before processing or storing it. For example, when creating a ticket, OmniDesk should verify that required fields contain valid information and that unexpected or invalid data is not accepted. 

- **SQL injection prevention:** The application must prevent users from inserting malicious SQL commands into fields that interact with the database. This helps protect the PostgreSQL database from unauthorized queries or modifications. 

- **XSS prevention:** The system should prevent malicious scripts from being inserted into fields such as ticket descriptions or comments. This is important because other users may later view this information through the web interface. 

- **CSRF considerations:** The application should protect actions performed through the web interface from being triggered without the user's authorization. This helps prevent an attacker from causing an authenticated user to perform unwanted actions. 

- **Rate limiting:** The system can limit the number of requests a user can make within a certain period. This can help prevent excessive requests and reduce the risk of abuse of services such as login or APIs. 

- **Backend authorization:** Security decisions must be made on the backend, not only in the frontend. Before allowing an action, the server should verify that the authenticated user has permission to perform it. 

- **Validation of user permissions:** The system should verify the user's role and permissions before providing access to specific functions or information. For example, an employee should not have the same permissions as a manager or administrator. 

These measures are especially important because OmniDesk uses role-based access control and the frontend does not directly access the database. 

The PostgreSQL database will also use constraints and validation rules to help maintain the consistency and integrity of the stored information. 

### **Privacy** 

Privacy refers to protecting the information handled by OmniDesk and making sure that users can only access information that they are authorized to see. 

OmniDesk may process different types of organizational information, including: 

- **Employee accounts:** Information used to identify employees and provide them access to the platform. 

- **User roles and permissions:** Information that determines what each user is allowed to see and do. 

##### **Software Projects Management** 

- **Support tickets:** Information about technical problems reported by employees, including their descriptions and status. 

- **Technical problems:** Details about errors, incidents, and solutions related to the organization's systems. 

- **Ticket history:** Information about the actions performed on a ticket, such as assignments, status changes, investigations, and resolutions. 

- **Attached documentation:** Files or evidence that users or technicians attach to tickets or technical documentation. 

- **AI-generated information:** Responses or recommendations generated by the AI assistant during the support process. 

This information should not be available to every user in the same way. Access should depend on the user's role and permissions. For example, an employee should only have access to the tickets and information that correspond to their responsibilities, while technicians and managers may require access to additional support information. 

OmniDesk will also maintain audit records to keep track of important activities performed within the system, such as changes to tickets, assignments, status updates, and AI-generated information. This provides traceability and helps determine what happened and when. 

In simple terms: the objective of privacy is to make sure that the right person can access the right information, while unauthorized users cannot access sensitive organizational data.

Model Update / Retraining Strategy 

The initial system will not require frequent model retraining. 

Instead, the knowledge base will be continuously updated as new validated technical information becomes available. 

##### **Software Projects Management** 

This means that when a technician validates a new solution, the information can become available to future AI queries without retraining the underlying language model. 

If sufficient historical feedback is collected in the future, the project could evaluate more advanced techniques such as fine-tuning or a specialized classification model. These would be considered future improvements rather than initial project requirements.

## **External systems and integrations** 

OmniDesk will interact with several external systems and services to provide some of its functionalities. These integrations allow the platform to communicate with external services without storing or implementing all of their functionality directly inside OmniDesk. 

The main external systems identified in the project are: 

#### **1. OpenAI API** 

The **OpenAI API** will be used for the AI Support Assistant. 

OmniDesk will send the user's technical question and relevant information retrieved from the knowledge base to the AI service. The AI will then generate a technical response with possible solutions or troubleshooting recommendations. 

##### **Example:** 

User's technical problem **>** OmniDesk **>** Knowledge Base **>** Relevant information > OpenAI API **>** AI-generated recommendation **>** OmniDesk. 

**Software Projects Management** 

The AI response will be returned to OmniDesk so that the user or technician can review it. AI-generated information must be identified and can be validated by an authorized user. 

#### **2. Microsoft Graph API** 

The **Microsoft Graph API** is identified as an external API that can be used to integrate OmniDesk with authorized organizational Microsoft services. 

This allows OmniDesk to communicate with external organizational services when required by the platform. 

OmniDesk **>** Microsoft Graph API **>** Authorized Microsoft service **>** Information / response **>** OmniDesk 

It will enable OmniDesk to communicate with external services the organization already uses, avoiding the need to maintain all that functionality within the platform. 

The document does not yet define exactly which Microsoft service will be integrated or what specific data will be exchanged. 

#### **3. Vercel** 

Vercel will be used as the hosting and deployment platform for the web application and backend services. 

This means that OmniDesk does not need to run exclusively on a physical server managed by the organization. The application can be deployed through the hosting platform. 

#### **4. Managed PostgreSQL** 

The project will use a managed PostgreSQL service to store the system's information. 

This database will contain information such as: 

- Users 

- Roles 

- Tickets 

- Priorities 

- Assignments 

- Statuses 

- Knowledge articles 

- Audit information 

The technology stack specifically identifies PostgreSQL as the database technology. 

User **>** OmniDesk Frontend **>** Backend / API **>** PostgreSQL **>** Data. 

##### **Software Projects Management** 

The frontend does not communicate directly with the database; the backend controls access to it. 

#### **5. Notification Service** 

OmniDesk will also use a Notification Service to notify users when important changes occur in their tickets. 

For example, notifications can be related to: 

- Ticket assignment 

- Priority changes

- Ticket resolution 

The Notification Service is identified as a system component that depends on Ticket Management and an external notification service. 

Ticket changes **>** OmniDesk **>** Notification Service **>** User receives notification.

## **Technical decisions and design rationale** 

The following technical decisions were selected for OmniDesk based on the project's requirements, expected workload, maintainability, and development team capabilities. 

|**Technical Decision**|**Rationale**|
|---|---|
|**Modular Monolith**|OmniDesk will be developed as a modular monolith|
